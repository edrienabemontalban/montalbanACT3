Abe's Burger

Ingredients

For the burger patty:

  • 250 grams ground beef
  • 1/4 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

For the burger:

  • 2 burger buns
  • 2 slices cheese
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato slices
  • Onion slices
  • Mayonnaise
  • Ketchup

Instructions

  1. Place the ground beef in a bowl.
  2. Add breadcrumbs, egg, soy sauce, salt, and pepper.
  3. Mix everything until combined.
  4. Shape the mixture into burger patties.
  5. Heat a pan and cook the patties until fully cooked.
  6. Place cheese on top of each patty and let it melt.
  7. Toast the burger buns lightly.
  8. Spread mayonnaise and ketchup on the buns.
  9. Add lettuce, tomato, onion, and the cooked patty.
  10. Cover with the top bun.
